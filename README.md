The first, AI-Cybersecurity_Maturity_Model _integration_Codebook_and_Dataset, contains the codebook and the verified dataset. The codebook sets out the forty-five indicators across the three pillars, each with its governance dimension, its wording as a scorable proposition, and the scoring rubric defining the five levels of the maturity scale and the five levels of the integration scale. The dataset comprises the five hundred and forty scored observations produced by applying the codebook to the twelve firms, presented as one worksheet per firm. Each observation records the level assigned, a summary of the disclosure supporting it, and the printed page reference at which that disclosure appears. Additionally, Indicators and framework mapping are given in the 'Indicator and Framework Mapping' worksheet for readers to get an idea on the derivation of the indicators.  

The second, AI_Cybersecurity_Governance_Self_Assessment_Tool, is the self-assessment tool. It allows an organisation to score itself against the same forty-five indicators, with the rubric visible alongside each item and results calculated automatically to produce pillar means, the maturity gap, integration by dimension, and a diagnosis identifying the weakest dimension.

A reader wishing to verify a specific finding should open the first file, select the worksheet for the firm concerned, and locate the indicator by its identifier; the evidence summary and page reference permit the score to be checked against the published annual report. A reader wishing to apply the model should open the second file and begin at the instructions worksheet.
